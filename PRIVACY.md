# BaliSurf Privacy Policy

BaliSurf does not collect personal data. No accounts, no login, no ads, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.

- **Anonymous usage analytics.** The app sends which screen you opened (e.g. "spots" or "spot/uluwatu") to our self-hosted [Umami](https://umami.is) instance at analytics.nextgensoft.co, together with the app version and your device model / OS version. Umami is cookieless and stores no persistent identifier: your IP address is used only to derive an approximate (country/city) location and a short-lived session hash, and is not stored. This data is never shared with third parties and is used only to see which parts of the app are used. Last updated: 2 October 2026.
- Forecast data is fetched directly from the free [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) API (CC BY 4.0). Your requests to Open-Meteo are subject to their [privacy policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms); the app sends only the surf spot's coordinates, never your location or identity.
- Session logs you record ("worked" / "didn't work") are stored only on your device and never leave it.
