BaliSurf gives you a straight answer for Bali, Nusa Lembongan and Lombok: is it worth paddling out, and when?

It does not try to out-forecast the big weather models. It pulls the same swell, wind and tide data everyone has — for free — and adds the thing generic apps lack: per-spot local judgement. Each spot has hand-tuned rules for the tide, swell direction, period and wind it actually needs, and the app turns the live forecast into a plain star rating, a one-line call, and the best window today.

Features:
• 20 spots across the region, grouped by coast: the Bukit (Uluwatu, Padang Padang, Bingin, Impossibles and more), Airport Lefts, the west coast (Canggu, Medewi), the east coast (Keramas, Nusa Dua, Serangan), Nusa Lembongan and Lombok (Desert Point, Gerupuk, Mawi).
• Star rating + plain-language verdict per spot ("Firing", "Fun", "Wrong tide").
• 24-hour timeline showing the best window to go.
• High and low tide times, free — many reefs here only work on a specific tide.
• Reef bathymetry maps derived from free satellite imagery, so you can see the bottom.
• Log whether a spot worked — your reports sharpen the forecast for everyone.

Free data from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Early access — spot rules are still being tuned with local surfers, and your session logs are how they get better.
